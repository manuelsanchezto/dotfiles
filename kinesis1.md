To change the sound:
 - Press "Prgm" + the key directly below it or the one below that one.
To change the mode:
 - Press Esc + w / p (theese two do not have super key available) / m (this one has)
 - After choosing the mode be careful to find other keys on the keypad layer (press keypad then whatever key)
